Choose a plan for your team's size and the number of tasks you run at once. Your hosted SuperPlane bill combines the plan fee with sandbox time and any SuperPlane-provided model tokens. If you connect your own API key or Codex subscription, that account covers model use while sandbox time still uses SuperPlane credits.

## Understand usage charges

The monthly plan fee and usage are separate. Buy credits for sandbox time and SuperPlane-provided tokens. Billing is centralized for your organization.

<div class="usage-metrics billing-usage not-content">
  <div><span>Sandbox time</span><strong>Charged separately</strong><p>Uses SuperPlane credits with every model source.</p></div>
  <div><span>SuperPlane-provided tokens</span><strong>API rate + 10%</strong><p>Applies to Pay-as-you-go, Starter and Team. Enterprise rates are custom.</p></div>
  <div><span>Your connected model account</span><strong>No token markup</strong><p>Use your Codex subscription on any plan, or your API keys on Team and Enterprise.</p></div>
</div>

### Sandbox time

Sandbox time covers the compute used to run your tasks. It remains a SuperPlane usage charge when you connect your own API key or ChatGPT subscription. Review sandbox consumption in your organization's billing settings alongside model usage.

### Model use

With SuperPlane-provided models, credits cover tokens at the provider's API rate plus 10% on Pay-as-you-go, Starter and Team. The pricing page lists models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, xAI Grok, DeepSeek, Qwen and Kimi. Enterprise model pricing follows your agreement.

With [your own API key](/billing/model-sources#compare-the-sources), your provider bills model tokens separately. With [your ChatGPT subscription](/billing/bring-your-own-subscription), Codex uses the task owner's connected account. SuperPlane adds no token markup to either option. Read [Model sources](/billing/model-sources) to choose a source for your workspace.

## Fund your usage

Top up your organization's credit balance when needed, or schedule automatic purchases. A Pay-as-you-go account has no monthly subscription fee, but it still requires a payment card and charges for usage.

Use [Costs and budgets](/billing/costs-budgets) to estimate spending, review repeated runs and configure the budget controls available on your plan.
