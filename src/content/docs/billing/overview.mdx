SuperPlane charges a fixed monthly fee for each organization. Machine use and managed model use draw from a shared allowance. You can invite your full team without a fee for each member.

Business includes a 14 day trial with no credit card required. You can connect private repositories during the trial.

## Understand usage charges

Your included usage covers machines and managed models. Usage above the included allowance is deducted from your prepaid balance. Purchased credits remain valid for 12 months.

<div class="usage-metrics not-content">
  <div><strong>$50</strong><span>shared each Business billing month</span></div>
  <div><strong>Provider rate + 10%</strong><span>for managed models</span></div>
  <div><strong>$0</strong><span>per member</span></div>
</div>

Managed model usage is available for models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, xAI Grok, DeepSeek, Qwen and Kimi. Enterprise customers can also use their own model API keys.

Unused monthly usage does not roll over. When the included $50 is exhausted SuperPlane deducts additional usage from your prepaid balance.
