SuperPlane charges a fixed monthly fee for each organization. Machine use and managed model use draw from a shared allowance. You can invite your full team without a fee for each member.

Business includes a 14-day trial with full access and no credit card required. If you don't subscribe when the trial ends, your workspace becomes view-only until you subscribe.

## Understand usage charges

Your included usage covers machines and managed models. Usage above the included allowance is deducted from your prepaid balance. Purchased credits remain valid for 12 months.

<div class="usage-metrics not-content">
  <div><strong>$50</strong><span>shared each Business billing month</span></div>
  <div><strong>Provider rate + 10%</strong><span>for managed models</span></div>
  <div><strong>$0</strong><span>per member</span></div>
</div>

Managed model usage is available for models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, xAI Grok, DeepSeek, Qwen and Kimi. You can use your own provider keys to avoid the 10% platform fee or combine managed and self-provided keys across workflows. Your provider bills usage on your own keys separately.

Unused monthly usage does not roll over. When the included $50 is exhausted SuperPlane deducts additional usage from your prepaid balance. You can top up your balance from billing settings.
